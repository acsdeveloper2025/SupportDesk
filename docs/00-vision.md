# Vision

SupportDesk enables enterprises to receive, prioritize, resolve, and learn from customer requests without compromising Tenant isolation or operational control.

## Outcomes

- Requesters can submit and follow support requests through web and email.
- Agents can triage, collaborate, meet SLAs, and communicate from one auditable workspace.
- Tenant Administrators can configure access, workflows, schedules, and reporting without vendor intervention.
- Platform Operators can run a secure, observable, recoverable service without viewing Tenant content by default.

## Success measures

Within 90 days of general availability: ≥80% weekly Agent activation among provisioned Agents; ≥70% Tickets resolved without reassignment; ≥95% of eligible SLA Targets calculated within 60 seconds; and ≥99% of notification jobs reach a terminal delivered or permanently-failed state within 15 minutes. Product metrics are Tenant-separated and exclude content.

## Scope

Initial scope includes Tenant lifecycle, identity and RBAC, Tickets, Comments, attachments, assignment, workflows, SLAs, email/web notifications, search, audit, reporting, administration, and operability. Billing execution, voice/chat, public marketplace, AI-generated responses, custom code execution, and cross-Tenant analytics are out of initial scope.
