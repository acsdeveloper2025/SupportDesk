# ADR-0003: Database strategy

Status: Accepted assumption pending OQ-04 and OQ-12 validation

## Context

SupportDesk needs transactional consistency for ticket writes, audit events, and outbox events while supporting large tenant volumes, search/report projections, and safe migrations. Exact cloud/database vendor choices remain open in OQ-12, and scale assumptions remain open in OQ-04.

## Decision

Use a transactional relational store as the authoritative system of record for tenant-owned domain data. Use projections for search, reporting, notifications, and analytics. Apply transactional outbox for external side effects and asynchronous processing.

The schema must support row-level tenant ownership, optimistic concurrency, immutable published configuration versions, append-only audit/outbox records, soft deletion, legal hold, and future partitioning.

## Alternatives considered

- Document database as source of truth: flexible but weaker for relational tenant constraints and workflows.
- Event sourcing as primary store: strong history but high complexity before domain volatility settles.
- Relational authoritative store with event/outbox projections: best balance for initial enterprise SaaS.

## Consequences

- Every material write must atomically persist domain state, audit evidence, and outbox intent.
- Search and reports are eventually consistent and must expose freshness.
- Migrations follow expand/migrate/contract.
- Future scale may require partitioning and aggregate/report projections.
