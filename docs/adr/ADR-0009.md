# ADR-0009: MVP SLA calendar and timer semantics (OQ-08)

Status: Accepted

## Context

SLA Targets must be calculated against Business Schedules. Open question OQ-08 asked which calendars, time zones, holiday sources, pause/reopen policies, and contractual semantics are supported. Multi-region calendars, external holiday feeds, escalation workers, and email alerts remain out of the first SLA engine slice, but policy selection and deterministic timers are required now.

## Decision

Adopt the following MVP semantics for Issue #26 (SLA Engine):

1. **One active published Business Schedule per Tenant**, keyed `default`. Additional schedules and multi-region calendars are deferred.
2. **Single IANA time zone** on each published schedule version. All due instants are stored in UTC.
3. **Weekly working windows** (Monday–Sunday) with local wall-clock start/end times. Overnight windows and zero-length (closed) days are supported.
4. **Holidays** are an explicit full-day date list on the schedule version. No external holiday providers.
5. **DST** arithmetic uses the IANA TZDB via the pinned `luxon` dependency.
6. **Pause:** `pending` pauses Targets when the captured policy has `pauseOnPending=true` (default true). `on_hold` pauses only when `pauseOnHold=true` (default false). `new`, `open`, and `solved` never pause.
7. **Response Target** starts at Ticket creation and completes on the first public Comment authored by a user other than the Requester.
8. **Resolution Target** starts at creation and completes on the first transition to `solved`. Reopen retains completion by default; `restartResolutionOnReopen` may start a new active resolution Target.
9. **Policy match:** first published policy by ascending unique Tenant priority; empty condition arrays match all values for that attribute.
10. **Breach/warning evaluation** runs synchronously on Ticket mutations and SLA status/timer reads. No background workers or email in this slice. In-app notifications are emitted once per Target threshold.

Existing Targets pin the policy and schedule versions used at selection. Silent rematch on priority/type/channel edits is forbidden; authorized recalculation remains future work (`SLA_RECALCULATION_NOT_ALLOWED`).

## Alternatives considered

- Multiple calendars per Tenant now: required for some enterprises, but deferred until admin UX and ownership rules exist.
- External holiday feeds: non-deterministic and vendor-locking without clear residency controls.
- Wall-clock-only SLAs: simpler, but contradicts documented Business Schedule-aware Targets (PR-05 / FR-08).
- Cron/worker breach detection only: idle Tickets would not breach without API traffic; workers are deferred, so sync evaluation on mutation and read is required for correctness in this slice.

## Consequences

- `luxon` is an accepted, pinned dependency for schedule arithmetic and must be justified in dependency review.
- Response and resolution Targets record exact business elapsed time and remaining windows.
- Email escalations remain deferred to future worker slice; in-app notifications are written directly to notification table.
